**Orchid Keys** is an original open-world crime game in 3D, set on a sun-soaked island city at golden hour. Explore five districts (Palmetto Heights, Meridian, the neon Lumen Strip, Coral Beach and the docks of Port Halcyon) on foot or behind the wheel: steal cars, dodge traffic and pedestrians, and outrun a 5-star OKPD response with cruisers that ram, roadblocks and a searchlight helicopter. Play Dez Navarro's seven-mission story *Salt & Neon* to win back the family beach bar from Councilman Shore, race the Sunset Sprint, run parcels for Coconut Couriers, and spend your money and respect at Gulfside Arms and Tiko's Body Shop. Everything (the city, cars, characters, story, synthesized sound and generated music) is made for this game; it runs from static files in `game/` with a vendored three.js build, no build step and no network.

Controls: WASD move/drive, mouse look, Shift sprint, Space jump/handbrake, F enter/exit/carjack, left click shoot, right click aim (and drive-by), Q/E or 1-5 weapons, R radio, H horn, M map, Esc pause. The game saves to localStorage (pause menu or Mama Ro's laundromat).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```
